Project 01 Tutorial

Connect an ESP32-D board to an NRF24L01 + PA + LNA radio module with seven jumper wires. Follow the steps in order, with the power unplugged until the last step.

What you need

  • 1 ESP32-D development board
  • 1 NRF24L01 + PA + LNA module with its antenna
  • 7 female-to-female jumper wires
  • 1 micro-USB cable

Step 1. Prepare the NRF module

  1. Screw the antenna onto the gold SMA connector first. Never power the module without its antenna.
  2. Hold the module like the picture, with the antenna on the right and the 2 by 4 pin header on the left.
  3. Find the seven pins you will use: GND, VCC, CE, CSN, SCK, MOSI and MISO. The last pin (IRQ) stays unconnected.

Step 2. Wire the module to the ESP32

Push one jumper wire onto each NRF pin, then connect the other end to the ESP32 pin in the same row of this table. The wire colors are the ones used in both pictures.

NRF pinWireESP32 pin
VCCRed3V3
GNDBlackGND
CEYellowGPIO 16
CSNGreenGPIO 15
SCKGrayGPIO 14
MOSIBlueGPIO 13
MISOOrangeGPIO 12
  1. Power first: red wire from VCC to 3V3, black wire from GND to GND. Use 3V3 only, never 5V.
  2. SPI lines next: SCK to GPIO 14, MISO to GPIO 12, MOSI to GPIO 13.
  3. Control lines last: CE to GPIO 16 and CSN to GPIO 15.

Step 3. Check and power on

  1. Compare every wire with the table and the ESP32 picture. A swapped pair is the most common reason a module does not respond.
  2. Make sure the antenna is tight and no bare metal pins touch each other.
  3. Plug the micro-USB cable into the ESP32 last.
  4. If the board resets when the module transmits, add a 10 µF capacitor between the module's VCC and GND pins, as close to the module as you can. The PA + LNA version draws more current than a basic NRF24L01.
